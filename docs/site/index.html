Your agents, side by side.
Your terminals, wherever you are.

XTermX connects you to your own terminal sessions. Run the Mac app or Linux node, follow your sessions from the companion apps, or use SSH to reach your own servers.

The Mac/Linux core is open source under MIT. The iOS and Watch applications are maintained separately. App Store availability will be announced when the product is released.

Download for Mac

Mac development build — Apple Silicon and Intel. Requires macOS 15 or later. This build is ad-hoc signed and not notarized by Apple; macOS may block first launch. iOS and Watch are not included.

Help with XTermX

For setup, pairing, file transfer or other questions, visit XTermX Support or email cassel@cassel.us.

Your data

Terminal connections go to hosts you choose. Read the iOS and Watch privacy policy for local storage, permissions and data transfers.
